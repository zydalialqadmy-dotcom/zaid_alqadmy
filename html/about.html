About Zaid Library

Zaid Library aims to spread knowledge and culture by providing diverse educational resources for all age groups.

  • Our Mission: Empower everyone to access knowledge easily.
  • Our Values: Quality, inclusiveness, innovation, community service.
  • Library History: Established in 2020, serving thousands of readers annually.

Our Team

The Zaid Library team consists of specialists in education and community service, working to develop content and provide the best services to visitors.

Our Partnerships

We collaborate with schools, universities, and training centers to spread knowledge and offer various educational events.

Our Achievements

  • Organizing more than 50 events annually.
  • Providing more than 2000 printed and electronic books.
  • Serving more than 5000 visitors every year.
